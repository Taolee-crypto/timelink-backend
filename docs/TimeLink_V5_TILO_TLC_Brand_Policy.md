# TimeLink V5 — TILO / TLC Brand Policy

Status: approved naming policy; implementation of user-facing labels remains to be audited.

- **Digital asset brand name:** TILO (Korean: 틸로).
- **Ledger asset code, ticker, and denomination:** TLC. Do not rename TLC database codes, balances, APIs, or transaction records.
- **Display examples:** TILO (TLC); 100 TLC (100 틸로). Do not assert market price, blockchain token issuance, or convertibility from this naming change.
- **Existing broadcast service:** TILO Radio (central AI radio). Distinguish radio service from TILO digital asset by context and label.
- **V5 economic ledger:** unchanged. TL categories and TLC remain distinct; no balance conversion or migration is implied.
- **Blockchain audit:** use the stable TLC asset code in hashes and canonical records. Brand names are presentation metadata, not ledger identity.
- **Rollout:** audit frontend copy, creator, wallet, admin and docs; replace asset branding only, not technical TLC identifiers or radio service names. Review trademarks and jurisdiction-specific token disclosure requirements before external promotion.
