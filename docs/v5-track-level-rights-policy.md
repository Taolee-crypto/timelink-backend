# TimeLink V5 — Track-level registration and rights policy

Status: approved product rule; implementation pending.

## Core rule
A rightsholder's membership never blocks registration of all their repertoire. Registration eligibility is determined **per recording**, not per person.

- A recording already registered on TimeLink cannot be uploaded as a duplicate, whether its rightsholder is a member or not. Link to the existing track and its sharing feature.
- An unregistered recording may be submitted by an ordinary member even when its rightsholder is already a TimeLink member. Submission does not itself authorize publication, streaming, or monetization; obtain required permissions first.
- An authenticated rightsholder may claim and manage only the recordings and rights shares they have substantiated. Being an authenticated member does not automatically grant rights over their entire catalog.
- If a track has been shared before the rightsholder joins or proves their rights, keep uploader attribution separate from ownership. Accrue rightsholder TL as payable-but-unclaimed, not uploader income. Disburse after approval and conflict checks.
- Distinguish compositions from sound recordings and rightsholder shares. Multiple legitimate owners can exist.
- Resolve duplicates using ISRC, audio fingerprint, and supporting metadata, with manual review for uncertain matches. Remasters, live versions, covers and distinct recordings must not be falsely merged.
- Flag disputes for review; suspend disputed payouts and affected publication permissions until resolved.
- Preserve V5 70% rightsholder / 30% platform allocation where applicable. Rights-holder TL cash redemption rules are separate from general listener rules.
- Record all decisions, evidence, approvals, and settlements in auditable records. Do not retroactively reassign already settled entries without explicit adjustment transactions.

## Creator Studio
Separate **Original Creation Registration** from **Existing Recording Registration**. Existing recording submission supports batches of up to 10; lookup metadata from Spotify and other authorized sources, but metadata is not proof of copyright or a usage license.

## Suggested acceptance tests
1. Verified artist owns 100 recordings, 20 registered: other 80 remain eligible for submission subject to permissions.
2. Existing track rejects duplicate submission by any user.
3. Rightsholder approval affects only explicitly verified track IDs and rights shares.
4. Unclaimed earnings never credit the ordinary uploader's wallet.
5. Recording matching is cautious when fingerprints/ISRC disagree.
6. Public playback is blocked until required usage authorization exists.
